## Decision

Code inspection corrected the initial hypothesis: casino cards do not select bets or place wagers. Keep navigation enabled during failed refreshes. Do not borrow sports selection-disabled rules for read-only links.

## Change

Replace CasinoHome's separate setInterval API poll with useSportsFeed(true). This shares its validation, sequential refresh scheduling, slow/error/retry state, last-successful timestamp and abort/late-response safeguards. Other casino data feeds remain outside this objective.

Extract the sports recommendation presentation into CasinoSports. It shows initial loading, slow-loading recovery, explicit first-load failure, later failure with retained-data notice, successful receipt time, ongoing update and valid-empty state. Retry uses the hook's retry callback and is disabled while retrying. Errors remain outside the busy card region so a slow request does not hide its status/retry announcement behind aria-busy. Accepted prices retain their supplied values; cards still show up to three fixtures and navigate through onSport(false). No quote, bet, wallet or account action is added.

Reuse existing sx-feed-error/sub/ghost/rowscroll/boost styles, including their mobile rules. No stylesheet or package changes.
