# 0082: Public market discovery

The public sports view exposes market identity, source and decimal odds. Selecting an odd asks guests to log in; only authenticated players mount the existing betslip and see match research actions. Removed the standalone Criar Aposta entry and duplicate match banner. Account, portal and authenticated transaction behavior is retained.

Em alta prioritizes live fixtures, then nearest scheduled kickoff, selecting three available markets across sports. Its caption describes this ordering without claiming measured popularity or trading volume. Prices remain sourced from validMarkets and the current feed.

The existing green brand accent is retained in dark mode. Surface tones become darker neutral green/charcoal, and mobile exposes the existing theme toggle. No new assets or dependencies.
