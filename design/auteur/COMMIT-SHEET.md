# COMMIT-SHEET — Candibet / Sports desk

## 1. Moment of care
A match remains in focus in the right research desk while a visitor compares other fixtures; when the feed fails, preserve the last successful board and disclose its age. No peak or scroll choreography.

## 2. Color
Restrained vermilion, oklch(0.54 0.20 28), below 10% of the working surface. Ground oklch(0.975 0.002 260), surface white, ink oklch(0.22 0.012 260). Target mean background L 0.94 on the default daylight workspace. The physical scene is following scores during the day on a laptop or phone; a light, high-contrast desk supports sustained reading. Red indexes live/selected controls with accompanying text, not promotional urgency. No cream, lavender, green-on-black sportsbook reflex or blue alternate re-skin. Dark mode will be charcoal with the same red roles and measured contrast, not a separate visual world.

## 3. Type
Archivo variable for broad, firm headings and match names; Source Sans 3 for humanist body text, labels and forms. Geometric/grotesque display versus open humanist text is the contrast axis. Actual sourced local woff2 files under assets/font, with licence ledger. Inter and the incumbent Geist are not selected: this is a new identity, with more open labels and sharper sports headings. Numbers are tabular, not a third mono face.

## 4. Grid break
Persistent 176px sports rail, flexible comparison board and 290px contextual research desk create an asymmetric working spread. A compact live score strip spans only the board; no twin feature-card banner. The account and cashier reuse this alignment instead of inventing a new dashboard.

## 5. Motion budget
Zero scroll-triggered families. Button color 120ms, dropdown opacity/transform 160ms, modal opacity/transform 220ms ease-out. Reduced motion removes displacement while retaining immediate state changes. The mockup needs no entrance motion.

## 6. Reflex check
a) Category reflex: dark green/blue canvas, promotional banners, tile stacks and tiny price labels. Local prior builds are direct evidence.
b) Avoidance reflex: an airy editorial landing page with a slogan and huge serif headline, making fixtures secondary.
c) Chosen deviation: daylight sports desk. Bold working headings, concise rows and a source-aware contextual research rail; density without a terminal costume. Moodboard tiles03/06 supply hierarchy and distinct column widths only. Broadcast tiles01/02/07 are rejected for chrome and bevels. No borrowed reference image ships.

## 7. House tells broken
1. Near-black default becomes a daylight silver/white ground at target L0.94; dark remains an optional functional theme.
2. Mono service labels become Source Sans 3 at readable 13–14px sizes; no technical-drawing voice.
3. Amber/acid accents become measured vermilion. No glow, scroll-instruction footer or oversized wordmark hero.

## Approval gate
A responsive, working design preview demonstrates sports/research, account/wallet, terminal and login before integration into React. User approval of this concrete artifact is required by Auteur's mockup gate; the prior permission to redesign does not choose this newly developed direction.
